# X Agents Pilot Offer Playbook

Status: draft for Rob review.
Created: 2026-05-21.

## Offer Name

`X Agents Pilot`

## One-Line Pitch

`A guided X Agents website pilot that helps visitors ask questions, leave details, and become qualified leads after hours.`

## Customer-Facing Description

X Agents Pilot adds a simple AI concierge to your website. It can answer approved questions about your business, collect lead details, and send your team a clean summary so fewer website visitors disappear without contacting you.

## Best Buyer

- owner-operated service business
- already has a website
- gets leads by phone or contact form
- misses after-hours inquiries
- wants more leads but does not want a complex software project
- can approve a small monthly pilot quickly

## Core Problem

Website visitors often leave because:
- they cannot find answers fast
- they do not want to fill out a form
- it is after hours
- they are not ready to call
- the site has no guided next step

## Core Promise

Not:
- "AI will run your business."

Say:
- "We help your website capture more qualified inquiries with a guided AI concierge."

## Included in Pilot

- one AI concierge experience
- one approved FAQ/knowledge set
- lead capture fields
- lead summary email
- basic qualification score
- weekly or monthly usage summary
- 30-day pilot review

## Not Included in Pilot

- full CRM integration
- SMS automation
- dispatching
- quote generation
- calendar booking
- unlimited custom workflows
- unlimited minutes
- compliance/legal advice

These can become Pro or Custom upgrades.

## Fair Use Language

`Your plan includes up to [X] AI conversation minutes per month. If usage approaches the monthly allowance, we will notify you before any upgrade or overage. This pilot is designed for normal website inquiry traffic, not high-volume support automation.`

## Lead Capture Fields

Minimum fields:
- name
- phone
- email
- service needed
- urgency
- city/service area
- notes
- best callback time

Optional fields:
- budget range
- property type
- existing provider
- how they found the business

## First Outreach Angle

Subject ideas:
- Quick idea for after-hours website leads
- Small AI concierge pilot for your website
- Are website visitors leaving without calling?

Short email draft:

```text
Hi [Name],

I noticed your website is set up mainly around calls and contact forms. We are testing a small AI concierge for local service businesses that can answer approved questions, collect lead details, and send you a clean summary when visitors come in after hours or are not ready to call.

This is not a big software project. It is a small 30-day pilot to see whether your website can capture more qualified inquiries.

Would you be open to a quick look at a sample?

Rob
AI Fusion Labs
```

## Qualification Questions for Sales Call

Ask:
1. How do most leads reach you today?
2. Do you know how many website visitors leave without calling?
3. Do after-hours inquiries matter for your business?
4. What questions do prospects ask before booking?
5. Who receives website leads today?
6. Would a clean lead summary by email be useful?
7. What would make a 30-day pilot worth keeping?

## Pilot Acceptance Criteria

A pilot is successful if at least one of these happens:
- captures leads that otherwise would have been missed
- improves speed to follow-up
- reveals common customer questions
- creates a repeatable FAQ/lead capture flow
- business owner wants to keep it another month
- business owner asks for deeper workflow integration

## Upgrade Paths

Upgrade to Pro when the client wants:
- SMS follow-up
- calendar booking
- richer persona
- multiple locations
- CRM/Sheet integration
- custom reporting
- multiple agents
- vertical-specific workflow automation
