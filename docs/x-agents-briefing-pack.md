## Core Product Thesis

X Agents are specialized, lifelike AI workers that use voice, video, domain context, approved knowledge, and configured workflows to create a more natural business interaction than a standard chatbot.

The strongest historical product line is:

> The agent is the interface.

That means X Agents should not be framed as "a chatbot added to a website." The intended advantage is a guided, human-feeling agent experience embedded in a business-specific flow.

## Current Safe Positioning

Safe description:

X Agents are interactive AI representatives for business websites, demos, and workflows. They can guide visitors, qualify leads, explain services, collect context, and hand off structured summaries when the required knowledge, tools, permissions, and workflows are configured.

Avoid claiming:

- unlimited autonomy
- fully general business operation
- guaranteed integrations
- guaranteed compliance
- final pricing
- production readiness for every demo
- current support for every historical agent or provider

Good safe language:

- guided experience
- interactive walkthrough
- voice/video AI representative
- business-tool connection when configured
- approved knowledge
- workflow handoff
- lead qualification and summary
- operator-approved outreach
