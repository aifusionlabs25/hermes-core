# X Agents 30-60 Day Revenue Plan

Status: draft for Rob review.
Created: 2026-05-21.

## Goal

Create first revenue inflow for X Agents within 30 to 60 days without waiting for the full long-term platform vision.

The immediate objective is not to prove every future X Agents capability. The objective is to package a narrow, sellable, useful pilot that local SMBs can understand, afford, and approve.

## Revenue Thesis

Most small businesses do not need a full autonomous AI platform on day one.

They need:

- more website visitors to contact them
- fewer missed calls
- better after-hours lead capture
- faster qualification
- a simple way to look more modern than competitors
- low setup friction
- low monthly cost

X Agents can start as a focused conversion product:

> A voice-enabled AI website concierge that greets visitors, answers approved questions, captures lead details, and hands off a clean summary.

This is a sub-product of the larger X Agents vision, not a replacement for it.

## Recommended First Offer

Working name:

`X Agents Lite`

Plain-English pitch:

`Add a voice-enabled AI concierge to your website so visitors can ask questions, get guided, and leave a qualified lead even after hours.`

What it does in v1:

- answers from an approved business FAQ
- captures name, phone, email, service need, urgency, and notes
- qualifies the lead with a simple score
- sends a summary to the business owner or office email
- optionally recommends booking or callback as the next step

What it does not do in v1:

- does not dispatch jobs
- does not promise 24/7 human-level support
- does not integrate with every CRM
- does not send SMS unless approved and configured
- does not make claims outside the approved FAQ

## Target Market

Start with low-friction local SMBs where a missed lead has obvious value.

Priority verticals:

1. Plumbing
2. HVAC
3. Roofing
4. Pest control
5. Garage door
6. Moving/logistics
7. Landscaping/pool service
8. Small law intake, only after compliance language is tighter

Best prospect profile:

- has a website
- phone-forward lead flow
- no chat or weak lead capture
- owner-operated or small team
- high value per booked job
- visible service area
- reviews/trust signals but poor conversion path
- likely loses after-hours leads

Avoid first:

- heavily regulated claims
- complex enterprise IT buyers
- customers needing deep CRM integration before paying
- businesses with no website at all unless sold as a separate web build

## Minute Pool Strategy

Rob's pooled-minutes idea is viable if treated as a controlled pilot economics model.

Core idea:

- buy or reserve a block of provider minutes
- sell small monthly subscriptions to multiple low-usage clients
- bank on average usage staying below included allowance
- monitor usage weekly
- upgrade or cap any client who becomes heavy usage

This can work if the product is sold as lead capture, not unlimited conversation.

Guardrails:

- never sell unlimited usage
- publish fair-use limits
- include a soft cap
- notify client before overage
- keep pilot plans month-to-month
- watch provider costs weekly
- track margin per client

Recommended pilot model:

- include 30 to 60 minutes per month per Lite client
- soft cap at 80 percent usage
- pause or require upgrade at 100 percent unless Rob approves overage
- use pooled minutes only after usage tracking works

## 60-Day Plan

By day 60, target:

- 2 to 5 paying pilots
- one repeatable vertical offer
- one working demo
- one lead tracker
- one usage/cost tracker
- one onboarding checklist
- one monthly report template
- one X-LINK feedback loop for product improvements

Minimum revenue signal:

- one client pays anything for the pilot

Good signal:

- 2 to 3 clients pay monthly and use fewer minutes than allowance

Strong signal:

- client asks for deeper workflow integration or refers another business

## Risks

Main risks:

- provider costs exceed subscription revenue
- setup work takes too long
- demos are unreliable
- offer sounds too advanced or too vague
- Rob spends too much manual time per client
- buyers do not understand voice AI value quickly

Mitigations:

- narrow the first offer
- cap minutes
- sell month-to-month pilots
- use simple FAQ-based knowledge
- avoid custom integration in Lite tier
- keep first vertical focused
- require Rob approval before any promise
