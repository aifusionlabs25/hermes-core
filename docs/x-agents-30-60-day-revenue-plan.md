# X Agents 30-60 Day Revenue Plan

## Overview
This plan outlines the rollout of the Full X Agents Pilot Program over the first two months. The pilot is a sub-product of the larger X Agents vision, not a replacement.

## Recommended First Offer
Working name:
`X Agents Pilot`

Plain-English pitch:
`Add a guided X Agents pilot to your website so visitors can ask questions, get guided, and leave a qualified lead even after hours.`

What it does in v1:
- answers from an approved business FAQ
- captures name, phone, email, service need, urgency, and notes
- qualifies the lead with a simple score
- sends a summary to the business owner or office email
- optionally recommends booking or callback as the next step

What it does not do in v1:
- does not dispatch jobs
- does not promise 24/7 human-level support
- does not integrate with every CRM
- does not send SMS unless approved and configured
- does not make claims outside the approved FAQ

## Target Market
Start with low-friction local SMBs where a missed lead has obvious value.

Priority verticals:
1. Plumbing
2. HVAC
3. Roofing
4. Pest control
5. Garage door
6. Moving/logistics
7. Landscaping/pool service
8. Small law intake (after compliance language is tighter)

Best prospect profile:
- has a website
- phone-forward lead flow
- no chat or weak lead capture
- owner-operated or small team
- high value per booked job
- visible service area
- reviews/trust signals but poor conversion path
- likely loses after-hours leads

Avoid first:
- heavily regulated claims
- complex enterprise IT buyers
- customers needing deep CRM integration before paying
- businesses with no website at all unless sold as a separate web build

## Offer Ladder
### Pilot Offer
Purpose:
- fastest path to first revenue

Suggested price test (example only):
- setup: $199 to $499
- monthly: $99 to $199
- includes a small minute allowance
- overage or high-usage upgrade required if usage grows

Deliverables:
- one website AI concierge
- approved FAQ
- lead capture form/summary
- owner notification email
- simple monthly report
- 30-day pilot review

### Pro Pilot
Purpose:
- higher-value businesses that want a stronger custom demo

Suggested price test:
- setup: $750 to $1,500
- monthly: $250 to $500

Deliverables:
- customized persona
- stronger vertical knowledge base
- richer lead qualification
- call/demo review
- follow-up copy drafts
- optional CRM/Sheet handoff

### Custom Build
Purpose:
- serious prospects with real workflows and budget

Suggested price test:
- setup: $2,500+
- monthly: $750+

Deliverables:
- custom workflow
- integrations

## 30-Day Timeline
1. Research and select 10 target prospects.
2. Run Local Lead Scout for each ZIP area.
3. Score leads and pick top 3.
4. Draft outreach and get Rob approval.
5. Conduct discovery calls and assess Pilot suitability.
6. Set up Pilot for agreed clients.
7. Weekly reviews of usage, leads, and feedback.
8. 30-day pilot review to decide continue, upgrade, revise, or pause.

## Metrics to Track
- number of leads captured
- conversion rate from lead to pilot signup
- minutes used vs. allowance
- revenue per client
- support time per client
- upgrade interest rate

## Risks and Mitigations
- Provider cost overruns: monitor minutes weekly, alert at 80% usage.
- Low conversion: select high-fit prospects, improve FAQ.
- Technical issues: keep pilot scope simple, use internal test environment.
